Why this shape

Free is a real product, not a teaser. One full AI-generated plan is enough to feel the “wow” and see exactly what more looks like — that’s the conversion engine and the demo hook at the same time.

Growth at $49 is the anchor. Starter feels limited, Scale feels like overkill, Growth feels right. At $49, a 25% affiliate cut is $12.25/referral/month recurring — worth promoting when customers stick around.

Ad-spend management starts at Growth. That module has real ongoing cost, so gating it protects margin and gives Starter users a clear upgrade path once they’re ready to run ads.

Scale anchors the ladder and opens a path for consultants and agencies — its presence makes Growth look reasonably priced, even if most people don’t need it yet.

Annual (~20% off) matters more than it sounds: upfront cash funds AI usage and runway, and people don’t reconsider every month.
