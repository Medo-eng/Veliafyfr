What it is

Not a one-shot business-plan PDF that disappears. Veliafy is an ongoing workspace: the AI has context on your idea, budget, stage, and progress, and stays available from first defining the concept through planning, building, and marketing once it exists.

What you get

AI assistant

Structured plans from your idea, follow-up conversation, and stage-aware tool recommendations — not generic templates.

Foundation

Capture your idea, niche, budget, audience, and stage so everything else in the product stays specific to you.

Organization

Boards, lists, calendars, docs, and a whiteboard — keep the work of building the business in one place.

Marketing & finance

Channel strategy, spend tracking, and a simple runway view so progress and budget stay visible together.

Who it’s for

People with a business idea who need more than a blank doc — a clear path, practical next steps, and guidance that adapts as the idea evolves. Built for early-stage builders who want one workspace instead of a pile of disconnected tools.
